YTSubsCore Terms of Service

Effective date: June 1, 2026

YTSubsCore is a private/internal personal tool operated by Michael Guber. It is not a public SaaS product and is not offered to external users.

Purpose

YTSubsCore reads YouTube subscription and video metadata for the authorized Google/YouTube account and sends private email notifications when subscribed channels publish new videos.

Permitted use

  • Private/internal monitoring of the operator's own YouTube subscriptions.
  • Read-only use of YouTube Data API endpoints needed for subscription notifications.
  • Local storage of limited metadata required to prevent duplicate notifications.

Restrictions

YTSubsCore does not permit or provide functionality for:

  • Uploading, editing, deleting, or otherwise modifying YouTube videos or account data.
  • Posting comments, rating videos, managing playlists, or performing YouTube write actions.
  • Selling, renting, or transferring YouTube API data to third parties.
  • Displaying advertising on or within YouTube content.
  • Providing analytics, reports, or dashboards to external users.
