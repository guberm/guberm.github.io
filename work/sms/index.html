SMS — Security Monitoring System

Vendor-neutral dashboard for 2000+ cameras, servers, motion detectors and serial controllers. Adaptive polling that won't flood your LAN, hysteresis instead of false alarms, Stream Lost tracked separately from Offline, pluggable serial protocol adapters, signed offline licenses — all on a single 4 vCPU box with zero cloud.

The problem

Your cameras, detectors and controllers already exist, often from different vendors — and monitoring software usually means replacing them or shipping the data to someone else's cloud. On a live site neither is an option: the hardware is wired, the network is shared, and an alert that cries wolf is worse than no alert.

What it does

  • Vendor-neutral dashboard for 2000+ cameras, servers, motion detectors and serial controllers
  • Adaptive polling that won't flood your LAN
  • Hysteresis instead of false alarms
  • Stream Lost tracked separately from Offline
  • Pluggable serial protocol adapters
  • Signed offline licenses

The whole system runs on a single 4 vCPU box, with zero cloud.Built and maintained by me. Licensed offline, no cloud account needed.
