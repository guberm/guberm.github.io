VAS — Video Analytics System

Upload recorded CCTV footage — get object detection and tracking, calibrated speeds, counting lines, license plates, and SAM2 click-to-track for any object. A ground-truth audit loop measures recall on your own footage; everything runs on-premise on CPU with zero cloud.

Open the live product → Discuss a similar system

The problem

Reviewing recorded footage is manual work: someone scrubs through hours of video to find one event, and nobody can tell you what the system missed. That is where analytics usually stops being useful — the numbers look impressive, but there is no way to check them against your own footage.

What it does

  • Object detection and tracking across recorded CCTV footage
  • Calibrated speed measurement for vehicles in view
  • Counting lines for entries, exits and pass-throughs
  • License plate recognition
  • SAM2 click-to-track: pick any object in a frame and follow it through the clip
  • A ground-truth audit loop that measures recall on your own footage

Runs on-premise, CPU only, no cloud service in the loop.Built and maintained by me. Licensed offline, no cloud account needed.
