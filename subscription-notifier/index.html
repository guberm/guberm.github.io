Subscription Notifier watches Michael Guber's own subscriptions and sends private email notifications.

The application uses the YouTube Data API with read-only OAuth authorization to list subscribed channels, check recent channel activity, fetch video metadata/duration, and send email notifications for newly published videos.

The application does not upload videos, modify platform data, post comments, rate videos, sell analytics, display ads, or provide a public user-facing product.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service Revoke Google access

API use

Scope: youtube.readonly subscriptions.list activities.list videos.list SQLite local storage Private email notifications

The application refreshes data every 10 minutes while running, processes only recent activity, and purges stored video notification metadata older than 30 days. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

Data retention

Stored video notification rows and stale latest-video pointers are deleted after 30 days. Subscription channel IDs/titles are retained only while the private notification service is active for the operator's account.
