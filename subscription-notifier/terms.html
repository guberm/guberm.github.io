Subscription Notifier Terms of Service

Effective date: July 3, 2026

Subscription Notifier is a private/internal personal tool operated by Michael Guber. It is not a public SaaS product and is not offered to external users.

Purpose

The application reads subscription and video metadata for the authorized Google account and sends private email notifications when subscribed channels publish new videos.

Permitted use

  • Private/internal monitoring of the operator's own subscriptions.
  • Read-only use of YouTube Data API endpoints needed for subscription notifications.
  • Local storage of limited metadata required to prevent duplicate notifications, subject to the 30-day retention limit described in the Privacy Policy.

Restrictions

The application does not permit or provide functionality for:

  • Uploading, editing, deleting, or otherwise modifying videos or account data.
  • Posting comments, rating videos, managing playlists, or performing write actions.
  • Selling, renting, or transferring API data to third parties.
  • Displaying advertising on or within API data.
  • Providing analytics, reports, or dashboards to external users.

Google terms

Use of Subscription Notifier with YouTube API Services is subject to applicable Google terms and policies, including:

  • YouTube API Services Terms of Service
  • YouTube API Services Developer Policies
  • Google Privacy Policy
